New Clothing Re-sale App, Ralphie’s Closet, Set To Launch for University of Colorado Boulder Students

Boulder, Colorado — September 18, 2025 — A new student-run startup, Ralphie’s Closet, today announced the upcoming launch of its clothing re-sale platform made exclusively for CU Boulder students. The app enables students to buy, sell, and trade clothing directly with peers on campus—no shipping, no guesswork, no outsiders.

“I love starting each semester organized, which means going through my closet and asking, ‘Do I really love this?’ … There had to be a better way, and the answer was right in front of me: 40,000 students all packed into one town, most with overflowing closets.”

Ralphie’s Closet keeps transactions student-to-student and local. Users log in with the CU Identity Key to verify campus affiliation, post items for sale, browse listings, and meet on campus to exchange clothes safely and quickly. This makes it easy to try things on before purchasing, avoid unreliable sizing, and save money.

The app supports sustainability by keeping clothing in circulation and out of landfills while reducing costs and environmental impact by cutting shipping and third-party fees. The team plans to roll out the app to CU students later this semester.
